KIM Collective

The Collective

Music in motion.
People in harmony.

KIM is a collective rooted in the idea of "Keepin' It Movin'"—using music to inspire movement physically, creatively, and personally. Drawing from jazz, funk, soul, rock, hip-hop, and a wide range of musical influences, the collective embraces exploration without being confined to a single genre. Every performance is an opportunity to create music that feels alive, connects people, and encourages them to keep moving forward.

At the heart of KIM is a shared commitment to creativity, collaboration, and continuous growth. Musicians come together to explore new ideas, learn from one another, and create meaningful experiences through music. Every gathering brings fresh perspectives while remaining connected by a common purpose: inspiring movement, connection, and growth.

KIM continues to grow through the people who bring their creativity, curiosity, and commitment to the music. If you're a musician in the Chicago area who feels connected to this spirit, we'd love to hear from you.
