About Mixolydian

Built for meaningful music, memorable meals, and genuine connection.

Mixolydian is a vision for an intimate jazz listening room where every detail is designed to bring people closer to the music. Inspired by Jacksonville's rich jazz heritage and the cultural legacy of LaVilla, the experience invites guests to slow down, listen deeply, and enjoy live performances in a setting that feels both welcoming and thoughtfully curated.

The idea began with three longtime friends brought together by a shared love of jazz, Southern hospitality, and meaningful conversation. What started as a simple evening of stories and imagination has grown into an evolving concept—one that celebrates exceptional musicianship, shared meals, and the kind of connection that can only happen when people gather with intention.
